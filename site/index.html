How it works

Rules decide who is at risk and why. AI only writes text and suggests tags, and a person confirms every step.

1Upload

Checks the sheet, columns and types, and rejects a wrong file with a clear reason.

2Tag notes

Each account manager note becomes a tag the rules can use. AI suggests tags for new wording, and a person confirms them.

3Score and rank

The same rules as the Excel workbook: four driver flags, a TPV alarm, value at stake and renewal urgency.

4Ranked list

All merchants, with the CSM 15 and the light-touch list highlighted.

5Merchant page

The reason, the evidence from 5 sources, the priority maths and the playbook.

6Draft

A stored draft, a DeepSeek email and WhatsApp message written from that merchant's row only, or a rules template.

7Fact check

Every number must come from the merchant's row; no promises, discounts or other merchants' names. Failed drafts are blocked.

8Approve

A CSM edits and approves. WhatsApp only with an opt-in. Nothing is sent, ever.

Guardrails

  • Rules rank, not AIFlags, scores and the order come from deterministic rules that match the workbook exactly.
  • A person confirmsEvery AI tag suggestion and every message waits for a person.
  • Nothing is sentApproving records the decision; the CSM sends from their own tools.
  • Synthetic data onlyNever upload real merchant data. Uploads stay in memory for the session.
